Figgy Saves His Own Skin

On Thanksgiving morning, Figgy went to Walmart to buy Chick-fil-A chicken nuggets. A Walmart employee caught him by the freezer, weighed him on the produce scale, and stuck a sale sticker on his tail. “I’m here for chicken,” Figgy said. “So are most people till they see the price,” she said.

Pearl, his guardian angel, circled the price gun. “Try Christmas,” she whispered. Figgy lit up his feathers. “Hear me out. You sell them a tree. Then you tell them it comes without the lights.” The employee peeled off his sticker. “Can you do that feather thing in the window?”

That’s how Walmart ended up celebrating Christmas for Thanksgiving. At noon, the manager asked everyone to name one thing they were thankful for. A cashier got as far as “My mom” before the loudspeaker cut in with “Jingle Bells.”

Figgy made his way to electronics. “I’m buying myself GTA 6 for Christmas,” he told Pearl. “And since somebody asked, I’m thankful for GTA 6.” She glanced into his empty basket. “What about the nuggets?” Figgy looked back at the employee holding the price gun. “Drive-thru.”
